\rsection{Summary}
Physicist turned embedded systems engineer.  Over ten years at Google building
custom camera hardware and firmware for real-world imagery capture: FPGA
soft-core firmware, precision time synchronization (IEEE~1588), high-speed
sensor data paths, and the bench tooling that brings new hardware up.
Ph.D.\ in physics with first-author papers in \emph{Nature Communications}
and \emph{Optics Letters} and a patented instrument.  Builds AI-agent-driven
engineering workflows (MCP servers, autonomous coding agents) around real
hardware.

\secsp
\begin{resitem}[2015--present]
\seclabel{Experience}{\bf Google LLC}, Mountain View, California\resdate\\
\textsl{Software Engineer, Geo.}  Embedded and systems engineer on the team
that designs and operates Google's custom camera platforms for capturing
ground-level imagery for Google Maps.
\begin{list3}
\item Own firmware for Xilinx FPGA-based camera systems: C/C++ on the
  MicroBlaze soft core, AXI-mapped peripherals (SPI, I\textsuperscript{2}C,
  MDIO, UART, GPIO), register maps, bitstream and ELF management, board
  bring-up, self-test, and validation over JTAG and UDP.
\item Lead precision time synchronization and its validation across camera
  networks (IEEE~1588 PTP, hardware timestamping, transparent clocks).
  Designed an offline timeline-reconstruction estimator (Kalman filter with
  Rauch--Tung--Striebel smoothing and Allan-variance oscillator models) that
  maps free-running sensor timestamps onto a grandmaster UTC timeline;
  characterized oscillator drift and thermal behavior in deployed units.
\item Built and maintain the team's camera development tool, a C++ CLI/TUI
  for hardware bring-up: register-tree and bus-topology browsing, self-test,
  usage telemetry, remote control over TCP/SSH, and an MCP server so AI
  coding agents can drive bench hardware directly.  Packaged for Debian.
\item Define wire formats and register layouts in Emboss (Google's open-source
  binary structure language) with generated C++ views; frame, metadata, and
  hardware-telemetry datagram protocols for 1G/10G Ethernet egress with DDR
  buffering, validated with capture-and-decode tooling.
\item Replaced a vendor-specific Ethernet PHY driver with a spec-compliant
  MDIO Clause~45 driver with deterministic link bring-up, isolating
  device-specific code paths ahead of an I/O board respin.
\item Introduced agent-driven development to the team: scheduled autonomous
  coding agents working a file-based issue queue behind test and lint gates,
  design-document review conventions, and local retrieval tooling over
  datasheets and register documentation.
\end{list3}
\end{resitem}

\begin{resitem}[2011--2015]
{\bf Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light}, Erlangen, Germany\resdate\\
\textsl{Doctoral researcher, Laboratory of Nanophotonics and Biosensing
(Dr.~Frank Vollmer).}  Theoretical and experimental research on
ultrasensitive label-free biodetection.
\begin{list3}
\item Invented the centrifugal force quartz crystal microbalance, a
  force-based biosensor that probes a sample's mechanical properties
  non-destructively in real time (\emph{Nature Communications} 2014; PCT

\item Discovered and modeled a new interference mechanism in conically
  scattered surface plasmon light, then used plasmon speckle to resolve
  single-nanoparticle events in an unmodified SPR setup (\emph{Optics

\item Designed and ran experiments with oligonucleotides, lambda DNA,
  self-assembled monolayers, and functionalized nanoparticles; built the
  optics, electronics, and data acquisition for each instrument.  Supervised
  a master's thesis (Jiapeng Huang, 2014).
\end{list3}
